# CareNest — User Guide

CareNest helps a family look after an older parent together, from anywhere: medicines,
appointments, caregiver visits, everyday tasks and help in an emergency, in one shared place.

> **CareNest is not an emergency service.** It alerts your family and caregivers. It never calls an
> ambulance, the police or the fire service. In an emergency, **call your local emergency number first**
> (112 in India and Europe, 911 in the US/Canada, 999 in the UK).

## 1. Getting started

1. **Create an account** with your name, email and a password of at least 10 characters (a short
   sentence works well). Choose your time zone and language.
2. **Create a care circle** for your parent — one circle per person being cared for. If you care for
   both parents, create two circles; you can switch between them at any time.
3. **Invite people.** Each person gets a role (below). Until email is set up on your CareNest server,
   copy the invitation link and send it yourself (WhatsApp, SMS…). Links work once and expire after 7 days.

A parent can also create their own circle and invite their family.

## 2. Roles: who can see and do what

| Role | Typically | Can |
|---|---|---|
| **Person receiving care** | Your parent | See everything about themselves, confirm medicines, ask for help. If they manage their own care, they decide what family can see. |
| **Family admin** | The organiser | Invite and remove people, edit the profile, add medicines, organise everything. |
| **Family member** | Siblings, relatives | See shared information, take on tasks and appointments, record medicines. |
| **Caregiver** | Paid or volunteer carer | See medicines and tasks, record visits and medicines given. |
| **Healthcare professional** | Doctor, nurse | See medicines and appointments, add medicines and notes. CareNest does not verify credentials. |
| **Emergency contact** | A neighbour | Only the emergency card and contacts, and emergency alerts. |

**Your parent's choices come first.** If your parent manages their own care, *they* decide whether
family can see their medicines, appointments and health details (Settings & privacy). Admins cannot
override this. Caregivers and healthcare professionals always see what they need to give care.

Temporary access (for example a relief caregiver for two weeks) ends automatically on the date you set.

## 3. Medicines

- Add each medicine **exactly as prescribed**: name, dose, times and days. CareNest never suggests,
  changes or "makes up" doses.
- Times follow **your parent's local time**. "08:00" stays 08:00 for them even if you live in another
  time zone; you see both times.
- Each dose shows what is known:
  - **Taken** / **Given** — someone recorded it, and the app shows who (your parent themselves, a family
    member on their behalf, or the caregiver who gave it).
  - **Skipped** / **Refused** — with the reason, if given.
  - **Not confirmed** — nobody has recorded it yet. *This does not mean it was missed.* Check with your
    parent or their caregiver. **Do not give an extra dose to make up for it unless a doctor or
    pharmacist says so.**
- Made a mistake? Use **Change this record** (within 72 hours); the original stays in the history.
- Enter how many tablets are left to get **Running low** alerts before you run out.

## 4. Appointments

- Add the time in your parent's local time, and questions you want to ask the doctor.
- **I'll drive** / **I'll go along**: say who is taking your parent. This is a promise between family
  members — CareNest does not book transport.
- If nobody has offered to drive three days before, the family is asked.
- Afterwards, **Record what happened**: what the doctor said, follow-up instructions, and next steps,
  which become tasks. These summaries are what a family member reported, not an official medical record.

## 5. Tasks

- Anything that needs doing: buying medicine, paying bills, booking a test.
- Tasks show **who has taken them** — or **"Nobody has taken this yet"**, so nothing is silently forgotten.
- **I'll do it** takes a task; **I can't do it** hands it back.
- Tasks can repeat daily, weekly or monthly. Overdue tasks are flagged to the person and the admins.

## 6. Caregiver visits and updates

- Caregivers tap **I've arrived** and **Finish visit**, noting how your parent was, a **handover note**
  for whoever comes next, and anything unresolved. The family gets a summary.
- Check-in times are what the visitor reports; CareNest cannot confirm someone was physically there.
- **Updates** are short notes for everyone. Mark one **important** to notify the circle. To fix a note,
  post a correction — the original stays visible, marked as corrected.
- The **Timeline** shows everything recorded, newest first, with who recorded it. You can search it.

## 7. Emergencies

On every page of a circle there is a red **Help** button.

1. **Call the emergency number** shown at the top — always the fastest way to get help.
2. **I need help** alerts the family. It asks you to confirm, so a stray tap doesn't alarm everyone.
   You can add a message and choose to share your location for this one alert.
3. People are alerted in the order you set under **Emergency contacts**: tier 1 first; if nobody
   responds within 5 minutes, tier 2, then tier 3, then everyone, then a final reminder.
4. Whoever is handling it taps **I'm responding** — the others are told, and escalation stops.
5. The request page shows who was alerted, who has seen it, and whether emails were delivered, so
   you can tell when nobody has responded. **Contacts without a CareNest account get no automatic
   alert** (there is no SMS yet) — phone them; the page lists their numbers.

Keep the **emergency information card** up to date (conditions, allergies, doctor, hospital, how to
get into the home). Everyone in the circle can see it, including emergency contacts.

Admins can send a clearly labelled **practice alert** to check the setup; it never escalates.

## 8. Notifications

- Notifications appear in CareNest (the number next to **Notifications**). If email is set up, you
  also get an email — without medical details; the details stay behind your sign-in.
- **Quiet hours** (Account) hold emails overnight. Urgent alerts (emergencies) are always sent.
- **Daily summary.** Everyday news (a visit that went fine, an ordinary update such as "I'm fine today")
  doesn't email you one by one. Instead, at 6 pm your time you get one email listing what you haven't
  already seen in the app — titles only, no details. Turn it off under Account → Notifications.
- Each circle lets you choose your own alerts (Settings & privacy → My alerts): medicine reminders,
  unconfirmed doses, running low, appointment reminders and driver requests, and unclaimed or overdue
  tasks. Alerts about something you've taken on yourself — a task assigned to you, a drive you
  offered — always reach you.
  Admins always receive urgent follow-ups if a dose stays unconfirmed and nobody is checking.
- Tap **I'm checking on it** on an alert so the rest of the family knows someone is on it.

## 9. Your data and privacy

- **Download my data** (Account → Your data): everything CareNest holds about you.
- Circle admins (and a parent managing their own care) can download the circle's complete record (Settings & privacy).
- **Delete my account** (Account) needs your password and typing DELETE. It is refused while you are the only person who can
  manage a circle — make someone else an admin first. Care notes you wrote stay in your parent's
  history, but your name is removed from them.
- The **Activity log** (admins and the parent) shows who changed what, so your parent can always see
  who looked after their information.
- **Signed-in devices** (Account) lets you sign out a lost phone or a shared tablet remotely.

## 10. Languages

CareNest is available in English, Hindi (हिन्दी), Telugu (తెలుగు) and Tamil (தமிழ்). Each person chooses
their own language under Account, so a parent can use Telugu while their children use English.
The Hindi, Telugu and Tamil wording has not yet been reviewed by native speakers; if something reads
oddly, please tell us.

## Getting help with CareNest

If something looks wrong, tell your circle admin. Admins: see `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md` for the person
running your CareNest server.
